Privacy Policy

PDFtools4u • Last Updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PDFtools4u ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our website (pdftools4u.in). Data privacy regulations require that we clearly communicate with website visitors about the data we collect and process, as well as inform you about your privacy rights.

2. Browser-Based File Processing

Supported PDF and image operations are designed to process selected files in your browser. For those operations, the file is read by the browser and processed on your device; the tool does not intentionally upload the file to PDFtools4u servers. This does not mean the website makes no network requests: it loads scripts and other assets, and analytics services described below may receive usage and technical information. Some features may download processing libraries or models. Review tool instructions and your browser's Network panel if you need to verify a particular operation.

3. Information We Collect

Hosting and delivery providers may process connection information such as IP address and request details. The site integrates Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity, configured to load after a visitor interacts with the page, and Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights. These services may process page views, interactions, performance, browser, and device information under their own policies and the site's configuration. Do not include sensitive document contents in feedback or messages to us.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The site uses scripts and similar technologies for analytics, performance measurement, and site operation. We do not currently serve advertisements or use Google AdSense. See our Cookie Policy for details. The site does not currently offer a per-category analytics consent control. You can block or delete cookies and other site data through browser settings; blocking analytics may not prevent all non-cookie requests made by third-party scripts.

5. Advertising

PDFtools4u does not currently display third-party advertisements or use Google AdSense. If advertising is introduced, this policy and the Cookie Policy will be updated to describe the advertising technology and applicable choices before it is enabled.

6. Your Privacy Rights (DPDP, GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, privacy laws may provide rights regarding personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below. Supported file processing is designed to happen locally in your browser; technical, analytics, and support data are separate and may be handled by the providers described in this policy. Using the site by itself does not satisfy a visitor's legal or regulatory obligations.

7. Data Retention

We base retention periods for analytics and usage data on legal requirements and legitimate business needs. When this data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not currently use AdSense or serve advertisements on the site.

9. Contact Us

Privacy inquiries: support@pdftools4u.in or https://www.pdftools4u.in/contact.
