Privacy Policy

PDFtools4u • Last Updated: August 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PDFtools4u ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our website (pdftools4u.in). Data privacy regulations require that we clearly communicate with website visitors about the data we collect and process, as well as inform you about your privacy rights.

2. Client-Side Processing (Your Files Are Safe)

PDFtools4u operates strictly as a client-side web application. All document processing, merging, splitting, compressing, and editing happens locally within your web browser. We do not upload, store, or transmit your PDF files or their contents to any external servers.

3. Information We Collect

While we do not access or collect your document files, we may collect standard internet infrastructure data, including IP address, browser type, device information, and operating system settings. We collect usage data via Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how our services are used.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and essential site functionality. We do not currently serve advertisements or use Google AdSense. See our Cookie Policy for details.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Data Sharing

PDFtools4u does not currently display third-party advertisements and does not currently use Google AdSense. We do not share personal information with advertising networks for targeted advertising.

6. Your Privacy Rights (DPDP, GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, data privacy laws grant you rights to access, restrict processing, or request deletion of personal data, and to opt out of sale or sharing of data for targeted advertising. We maintain a strict zero-data-retention policy for processed files.

7. Data Retention

We base retention periods for analytics and usage data on legal requirements and legitimate business needs. When this data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at minors. We set an age threshold of 13 years old and do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Contact Us

Privacy inquiries: support@pdftools4u.in or https://www.pdftools4u.in/contact.
