1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how PDFtools4u uses cookies and similar technologies on pdftools4u.in. Read it with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and scripts used to remember preferences or measure how a site is used.

3. Document Files Are Never Stored in Cookies

PDF, image, and document processing runs locally in your browser. Your files are not uploaded to our servers and are not written into cookies.

4. Cookies We Use

  • Essential / functional: Needed for basic site behaviour, such as remembering UI state during a session.
  • Analytics: After you first interact with the site, we may load Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to measure visits and improve the product.
  • Advertising: We do not currently serve advertisements or set advertising cookies.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Analytics cookies may be set by Google and Microsoft. See the Google Privacy Policy and Microsoft Privacy Statement.

6. How to Control Cookies

You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking analytics cookies does not stop PDF or image tools from working.

7. Updates

If we introduce advertising or new cookie categories, we will update this Cookie Policy first.
